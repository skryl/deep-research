## Business

Kilroy Realty is a West Coast office and life-sciences REIT with ~17 million square feet across the **San Francisco Bay Area, Greater Los Angeles, San Diego, Seattle, and Austin**. The portfolio skews toward Class A office and life-sciences buildings concentrated in submarkets favored by venture-backed tech, biotech, and increasingly **AI labs**.

The post-2022 office-REIT downturn hit Kilroy along with peers, but the SF Bay Area specifically has begun to tighten in 2024–2025 as well-funded AI lab tenants (OpenAI, Anthropic, Scale AI, Mistral US, xAI Bay Area, and the long tail of well-funded foundation-model startups) sign large gross leases at top-tier buildings.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

The fund has not publicly explained the position. The most coherent reading is:

- **AI tenant absorption tightens specific submarkets** even while the broader office market stays weak — a "distortion" trade rather than a directional office bet.
- **Kilroy's Bay Area / SF concentration** is the cleanest expression among public office REITs of "AI-tenant-heavy submarket".
- **The position may be paired** with shorts on generic office REITs in the fund's non-13F book — a long-short pair-trade expression.

At <1% of the 13F, this is the smallest long position in the portfolio and is best read as a thematic side-bet rather than a core holding.

## Risks

- **Office demand outside AI tenants remains weak** in many KRC submarkets.
- **Interest-rate sensitivity** — REIT cap rates have been volatile.
- **AI-tenant-concentration risk cuts both ways** — if AI startup funding contracts, the marginal demand evaporates.
- **Possible misreading of intent** — without disclosure, the actual rationale is uncertain.

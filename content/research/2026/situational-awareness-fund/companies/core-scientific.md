## Snapshot

- **Ticker:** CORZ (Nasdaq)
- **Bucket:** [Data-Center Operators (HPC Pivots)](research/2026/situational-awareness-fund/categories/data-centers)
- **Q4 2025 fund position:** $418.7 M common (28.76 M sh) — **7.59 % of 13F**, **#6 holding**

## Business

Core Scientific operates large-scale data-center campuses across Texas, Oklahoma, Georgia, North Dakota, and North Carolina, with ~1.2 GW of energized power capacity and additional contracted/under-construction capacity bringing the long-term plan toward 2+ GW. The business is mid-pivot from a Bitcoin self-mining + miner-hosting model (its origin) toward HPC/AI hosting, anchored by a series of contracts with CoreWeave that — when fully built out — represent the largest non-hyperscaler hosting commitment publicly disclosed.

The company emerged from Chapter 11 bankruptcy in January 2024 with a clean balance sheet, large NOLs, and ownership of physical assets that the AI buildout subsequently revalued sharply. The 2024–2025 CoreWeave hosting contracts (~590 MW across multiple sites, expandable, with 12-year terms and ~$10 B+ aggregate contracted revenue) re-rated the equity from a distressed miner into a thinly-disguised data-center developer.

## CoreWeave Contract Mechanics

The CoreWeave deals work approximately like this: CORZ provides the building, power infrastructure, cooling, security, and operational staff under a long-dated take-or-pay contract; CoreWeave brings the GPUs and runs the workload. CORZ revenue per MW is in the low-to-mid hundreds of thousands per year — less than CoreWeave earns per MW from end customers, but stable, contracted, and high-margin relative to the volatile mining business it replaces.

## Financial Trajectory

| Period | Revenue | Mix |
|--------|--------:|-----|
| 2023 FY | ~$502 M | Almost entirely Bitcoin mining + hosting |
| 2024 FY | ~$510 M | Mining + first stub of HPC hosting |
| 2025 FY | reported ~$700 M+ | First full year of CoreWeave HPC ramp |
| 2026 run-rate | guided >$1.5 B at full HPC build-out | HPC majority |

Margins shift dramatically with the mix: HPC hosting is contracted high-80s gross; Bitcoin mining is volatile and tied to BTC price.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

CORZ is the *anchor* of the data-centers bucket and arguably the model the fund believes other miners will follow. Its CoreWeave contracts demonstrate the re-rating mechanism: stranded power capacity, valued at miner multiples, gets repriced at data-center multiples once contracted to an AI tenant. The fund grew the position from ~6 M sh on entry to ~28.8 M sh by Q4 2025 — a ~5x size increase that signals it as the highest-conviction name in the bucket.

## Risks

- **CoreWeave counterparty risk** — concentration in a single (also-fund-owned) customer.
- **HPC retrofit execution.** Mining sites typically lack tier-III/IV uptime, redundant fiber, and liquid cooling. Conversion costs and timelines have slipped at peers.
- **Bitcoin price downside** in the residual mining segment, which still produces meaningful revenue and EBITDA.
- **Power-contract renegotiation risk** if existing PPAs were written for mining-only use.

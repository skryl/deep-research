## Business

Babcock & Wilcox is a 150-year-old industrial-power company best known for **utility-scale boilers and steam-generation systems**. Modern segments include thermal (boilers, retrofits, environmental controls for fossil and biomass plants), renewable (waste-to-energy, biomass), and a small but strategically interesting **BWXT-spin small modular reactor (SMR)** lineage — though commercial SMR business sits with the separately listed BWX Technologies.

In the AI-power context, B&W's relevance is twofold: (1) coal-plant life-extensions are seeing renewed interest as utilities defer retirements to meet AI load growth, and B&W performs the controls/retrofit work; (2) any SMR or advanced-nuclear deployment for AI data centers would benefit B&W-adjacent supply chains.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

B&W is the *speculative* end of the power bucket. Most of the AI-power-related deal flow is several years out, but if utilities meaningfully extend coal life or SMRs reach commercial deployment for data centers, B&W has unique installed-base relationships. The fund's tiny position size reflects the speculative, long-duration nature of the bet.

## Risks

- **Balance sheet** has been stressed for years; refinancing risk.
- **Coal-to-gas transition** continues to reduce B&W's largest legacy market.
- **SMR commercial deployment** remains slow — too speculative to underwrite today.

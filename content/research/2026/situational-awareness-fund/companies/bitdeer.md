## Business

Bitdeer was spun out of Bitmain (the dominant ASIC manufacturer) in 2023 and went public via SPAC in 2024. It operates Bitcoin mining sites in **Norway, Bhutan, the United States, and Canada**, and additionally develops its own ASIC mining hardware (the SEAL chip family) — a unique vertical-integration story in the bucket.

In 2025 Bitdeer launched an AI Cloud product targeting NVIDIA H100/H200 inference workloads, leveraging spare power capacity at its Norway site (cheap hydro, naturally cool). This is a very early-stage adjacency.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

Bitdeer is the bucket's "unconventional" expression: international power positions (Bhutan hydro is unique among public miners), in-house ASIC capability that could be repurposed for inference accelerators, and a small AI cloud pilot. The minimal position size reflects the speculative nature — useful diversification within the bucket but not a core conviction.

## Risks

- **Bitmain control overhang** in float and governance.
- **Bhutan and Norway operational risk** — small-country regulatory exposure.
- **In-house ASIC strategy** is capital intensive and competes with Bitmain itself.

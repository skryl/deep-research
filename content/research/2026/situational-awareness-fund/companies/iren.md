## Business

IREN is an Australian-headquartered, US-listed data-center operator with sites in British Columbia (Canada — hydro-powered), West Texas (Childress and Sweetwater — primarily wind/solar with grid backup), and a smaller Prince George BC campus. Total energized capacity is around 810 MW with a build path toward >2.9 GW including the Sweetwater Texas campus.

The company runs a mixed model: Bitcoin self-mining at most existing capacity, plus a fast-growing AI Cloud / HPC business launched in 2024 that monetizes a portion of the Texas fleet via NVIDIA H100 / H200 / B200 / GB200 GPUs leased on-demand or under multi-year contracts. Unlike CORZ which is primarily a hosting business (real estate + power), IREN's AI cloud product competes more directly with CoreWeave-style on-demand GPU rental — using GPUs IREN owns on its balance sheet.

## Sweetwater and the HPC Site

The Sweetwater Texas campus, announced in 2024 with phased buildout through 2027, is being designed from inception for HPC tenancy: liquid-cooled, redundant fiber, tier-III uptime, with reportedly multiple GW of contracted power. It is the most ambitious greenfield HPC build in the bucket and the primary asset behind the fund's conviction.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

IREN expresses the bucket thesis through a slightly different mechanism than CORZ: rather than only renting space, it owns GPUs and rents compute. This captures more of the value chain (and more of the risk). The fund owning both CORZ and IREN suggests deliberate diversification across "real-estate-only" and "real-estate + GPUs" expressions of the same theme.

## Risks

- **Capex intensity.** Sweetwater build is reportedly $5 B+ over multiple years; financing risk if AI demand softens before full lease-up.
- **Bitcoin exposure.** Roughly half of revenue still tied to BTC price.
- **Australian tax / dual-listing complexity** in valuation.
- **Competition from CoreWeave**, which has access to similar NVIDIA allocations and arguably better customer relationships.

## Business

Hut 8 is the post-merger entity formed in late 2023 by combining the original Canadian Hut 8 mining business with the US Bitcoin Corp. mining and hosting business. It operates sites in Alberta, Ontario, Texas, New York, and Nebraska. Total energized capacity is roughly ~1.3 GW including the Vega site in Texas (200 MW, designed for HPC/AI).

The most distinctive corporate move in the bucket: Hut 8 stood up **Highrise AI**, a dedicated AI/HPC subsidiary in 2024, with NVIDIA H100 deployments and an enterprise GPU-cloud product. This is the most committed organizational pivot of any name in the bucket — a public statement that the company sees AI hosting as the future business rather than a side bet.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

Hut 8 fills a smaller-cap niche in the basket: clear pivot intent, executable scale, but smaller balance sheet than CORZ/IREN/APLD. The fund's small position size (<1% of 13F) is consistent with treating it as a "completeness" name rather than a high-conviction expression — buying the whole bucket diversifies which name signs the next CoreWeave-style deal.

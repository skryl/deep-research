## Business

ProPetro is a Permian-focused pressure-pumping and well-services company with a fleet that has been progressively electrified through the **PROPWR** (electric frac) and ancillary power-generation initiatives. Like Liberty, ProPetro has begun to monetize the natural-gas-fired generation equipment that powers its frac fleet by also offering it for behind-the-meter data-center power and other temporary-power applications.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

ProPetro is the **lower-conviction sibling** to Liberty in the Wright/oilfield-services-to-power adjacency basket. The fund holds both at very small size — a bet that the broader oilfield-services-to-distributed-power crossover will create incremental value as data centers compete for the same gen-set inventory that frackers use.

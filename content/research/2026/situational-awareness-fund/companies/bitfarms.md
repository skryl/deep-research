## Business

Bitfarms operates Bitcoin mining sites across Canada (Quebec), the US (Pennsylvania, Washington State), Argentina, and Paraguay. After a contested 2024 governance battle (the Riot acquisition attempt was rebuffed), new CEO Ben Gagnon took over with an explicit mandate to pivot the Pennsylvania **Sharon site** toward HPC tenancy.

The Pennsylvania site sits on PJM Interconnection grid — one of the most desirable US power markets for AI buildout — and has expansion potential to ~1 GW. It is the asset on which the bucket thesis on Bitfarms rests.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

Bitfarms is the smallest by market cap and least-far-along in the conversion of any Q4 2025 holding in the bucket. Position size reflects a "buy the option" sizing: small dollars relative to the upside if the Sharon site signs an anchor tenant.

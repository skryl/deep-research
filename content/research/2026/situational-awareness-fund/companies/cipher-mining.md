## Business

Cipher Mining was spun out of crypto-trading firm Bitfury in 2021 and went public via SPAC. It operates Bitcoin mining sites primarily in West Texas, with an aggregate hashrate among the top ten US public miners. The strategically interesting asset is the **Black Pearl** site near Stockdale TX — a ~300 MW campus that includes infrastructure suitable for, or convertible to, HPC tenancy.

Throughout 2025 the company publicly disclosed it was in advanced HPC-hosting discussions with hyperscalers and AI cloud providers, with at least one preliminary letter-of-intent reported. As of early 2026 no anchor HPC contract had been signed and announced — Cipher remains a "next miner to convert" candidate.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

Cipher is a mid-sized option in the bucket. It owns reasonably good West Texas power positions (cheap, abundant, increasingly contested) and has been more publicly vocal about HPC ambitions than peers like Marathon. The fund's $154 M position size suggests it is treated as a moderate-conviction expression of "miners likely to sign HPC deals in 2026", rather than a high-conviction anchor like CORZ or APLD.

## Risks

- **No HPC contract signed** as of Q4 2025 — the bucket thesis on Cipher is forward-looking.
- **West Texas power competitive intensity** — every major miner and several hyperscalers (and the Stargate sites) are competing for the same grid capacity.
- **Bitcoin price** still drives most of the cash flow.

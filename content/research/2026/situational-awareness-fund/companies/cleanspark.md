## Snapshot

- **Q4 2025 fund position:** $16.6 M (1.64 M sh) — **0.30 % of 13F**

## Business

CleanSpark is a US-only Bitcoin miner, with sites concentrated in Georgia, Tennessee, Mississippi, New York, and Wyoming. Total energized capacity is ~1 GW. Among the bucket, CleanSpark is the *least* committed to an HPC pivot — management has explicitly described the company as a Bitcoin-mining specialist and resisted reframing it.

CleanSpark has, however, opened the door to selling spare power capacity or hosting third-party HPC at sub-scale. As of Q4 2025 no major HPC contract has been announced.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

The fund's small CleanSpark position is a "completeness" buy — owning every meaningful US-listed Bitcoin miner with substantial energized capacity ensures that whichever name pivots first, the bucket benefits. CleanSpark is the bucket's hedge against being wrong about which specific names pivot.

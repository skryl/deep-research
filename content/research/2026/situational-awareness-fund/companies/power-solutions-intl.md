## Business

PSIX designs and manufactures industrial power-system engines (largely large-displacement spark-ignited engines running on natural gas, propane, or biogas). Applications include forklifts, industrial sweepers, mobile equipment, and — most importantly for the fund's thesis — **stationary gen-sets for backup, prime, and continuous power generation**.

The data-center industry consumes large numbers of these engines: every tier-III/IV facility deploys multi-MW of redundant gen-set capacity for backup, and an increasing number deploy them for *prime* power as utility interconnects lag.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

PSIX is a small, indirect bet on the *gen-set engine* layer of the data-center supply chain. While Bloom and Solaris get the spotlight, every conventional gen-set deployed at a data-center site contains an engine — and PSIX is one of the few US-listed pure-plays on the engine itself rather than the assembled gen-set (which would be a Caterpillar, Cummins, Generac, or Kohler box).

## Business

Riot is one of the largest US public Bitcoin miners by hashrate, operating principally at two Texas campuses: **Rockdale** (legacy, ~700 MW) and **Corsicana** (the larger newer site, expansion path to ~1 GW). The company also includes an electrical-equipment subsidiary (ESS Metron) that builds custom switchgear for data centers, both internally and for third parties.

In 2024–2025 Riot publicly opened the door to HPC conversion at the Corsicana site, hiring data-center industry executives and engaging consultants on tenant attraction. As of Q4 2025 no signed HPC anchor tenant had been announced.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

Riot is a "scale + optionality" position in the bucket: large hashrate provides decent baseline cash flow during BTC up-cycles; Corsicana provides a multi-GW optionality on AI conversion. The smaller fund position size relative to CORZ, IREN, APLD reflects that Riot is later in the conversion process and has historically been more committed to staying a pure miner.

The ESS Metron switchgear subsidiary is an under-appreciated source of incremental data-center exposure: data-center-grade switchgear lead times are 18–30 months, making in-house production a meaningful competitive advantage if Corsicana converts.

## Risks

- **Slowest pivoter** in the bucket — management has been least vocal about HPC strategy.
- **Activist overhang** — multiple activist campaigns (including reported Starboard involvement) push for HPC monetization but raise governance noise.
- **Power-cost exposure** to ERCOT volatility.

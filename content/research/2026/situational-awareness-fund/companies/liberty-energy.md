## Business

Liberty Energy is a leading US oilfield-services pressure-pumping company — providing hydraulic-fracturing fleets primarily to Permian Basin operators. Founded by **Chris Wright** (CEO until his confirmation as US Energy Secretary in January 2025), Liberty has been an outspoken thought-leader on the gas-and-power thesis: that natural gas is the long-duration solution to both energy poverty and AI-driven electricity demand.

Liberty operates next-generation electric and dual-fuel frac fleets that dramatically reduce diesel consumption, and through its **Liberty Power Innovations (LPI)** subsidiary supplies natural-gas-powered behind-the-meter generation equipment — including for data centers.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

The fund's Liberty position is a small "Wright thesis" expression: a bet on the political-industrial alignment under the new US Energy Department leadership that gas, gen-set, and on-pad-power will be favored over alternatives. LPI is the data-center-relevant subsidiary; the broader frac business provides the cash flow that funds it.

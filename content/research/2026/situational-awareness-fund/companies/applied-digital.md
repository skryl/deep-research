## Business

Applied Digital was founded as a Bitcoin-mining-hosting business but has pivoted hardest among its peers toward dedicated AI/HPC data-center development. Its flagship Ellendale, North Dakota campus — sited on top of cheap stranded wind generation — was designed from the start for liquid-cooled HPC tenancy and is the basis of the multi-year, multi-billion-dollar lease deal with **CoreWeave (~250 MW initial, expandable to ~400 MW)** announced in 2024.

The company also operates a smaller HPC cloud business of its own at modest scale and retains a residual mining-hosting segment in the legacy Jamestown ND site.

## Ellendale and the CoreWeave Lease

The Ellendale campus is the most-watched HPC retrofit / build in the bucket. Phase I (~100 MW) is operational with CoreWeave installed; phases II and III are under construction. The contracted revenue (12-year terms, ~$7 B aggregate at full build) materially exceeds Applied Digital's prior enterprise value, which is the source of the 2024–2025 re-rating. Project financing for the remaining phases — including the 2025 Macquarie infrastructure equity investment — has been a key catalyst.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

APLD is the cleanest "stranded power converted to AI hosting" expression in the portfolio: a single transformative customer (CoreWeave), a single transformative site (Ellendale), and a clear yardstick to mark the trade against. The fund holds it alongside CORZ as the two largest non-CoreWeave names in the data-centers bucket.

## Risks

- **Single-customer concentration** — CoreWeave is essentially the entire HPC revenue line.
- **Construction execution and financing** for Ellendale phases II/III; cost overruns or delays compress IRR.
- **Power-grid and curtailment risk** in Western North Dakota.
- **CoreWeave counterparty risk** flows through to APLD via the lease.

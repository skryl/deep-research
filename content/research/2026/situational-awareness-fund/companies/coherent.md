## Business

Coherent (formed by II-VI's 2022 acquisition of Coherent Inc.) is a diversified photonics and lasers company. The portfolio spans:

- **Datacom transceivers and optical components** for hyperscaler data centers — most relevant to the AI thesis.
- **Telecom transmission components** for long-haul and metro networks.
- **Industrial lasers** for materials processing.
- **Consumer optoelectronics** including some Apple-related products.
- **SiC (silicon carbide) substrates** for power electronics — a smaller but high-growth segment.

The 800G transceiver opportunity, datacenter-interconnect (DCI) coherent optics, and the shift to higher-power lasers for advanced packaging all favor Coherent in the AI cycle.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

Coherent overlaps Lumentum on the cleanest "AI optics" line (datacenter transceivers and laser components) but is more diversified across industrial and telecom segments. The fund's position is sized at roughly 1/5 of Lumentum — explicit signal that it prefers the purer optics pure-play but values Coherent for completeness and for its slightly different end-market mix.

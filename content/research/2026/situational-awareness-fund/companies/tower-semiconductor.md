## Business

Tower Semiconductor is an Israeli specialty foundry (formerly TowerJazz) operating fabs in Israel, the US, Italy, and Japan. Tower runs **mature and specialty nodes** — RF SOI, BCD power, mixed-signal CMOS, image sensors, MEMS — that the leading-edge foundries (TSMC, Samsung Foundry, Intel Foundry) have largely exited or never operated. Customers include analog/RF, sensor, and power-management chip designers across automotive, industrial, mobile, and increasingly **AI-adjacent power management**.

Intel attempted to acquire Tower in 2022 for $5.4 B but the deal was abandoned in August 2023 after Chinese regulatory approval timed out. The two companies subsequently announced a manufacturing partnership: Tower operates an Intel-built 300mm fab in New Mexico, providing US capacity for Tower's specialty processes — a meaningful onshoring of analog/RF/specialty silicon to the US.

## Why It Fits the Thesis

AI accelerators (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel) need a long tail of *companion* silicon — high-side gate drivers, voltage regulators, isolators, RF front-ends, sensors. Most of that is fabbed at specialty foundries; Tower is one of the largest US-accessible options. As the AI build-out scales, the demand for these companion chips scales with it, even if Tower doesn't make any AI accelerators itself.

The fund's modest position is consistent with Tower being a *specialty-foundry onshoring* expression — a side bet that complements the Intel call position rather than overlapping with it.

## Risks

- **Israeli geopolitical exposure** — main fabs are in Israel.
- **Specialty-foundry margin profile** is structurally lower than logic foundries.
- **Customer concentration** in a few large analog/sensor customers.

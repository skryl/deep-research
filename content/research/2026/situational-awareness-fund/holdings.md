### Notes on the table

- **Option lines** (CRWV, INTC, EQT, BE calls; INFY puts) are reported at the **value of the underlying shares**, not premium paid. Real cash-at-risk is a small fraction of the line's headline value.
- **The single share of INTC common (line 29, value $37)** is a tracking placeholder used to satisfy the SEC convention that a CUSIP appearing as an option also appear as a common-stock line.
- **Infosys puts** are the only short disclosure in Q4 2025. Q3 2025 had additional puts on AVGO, CRWV, MU, NVDA, TSM, and the VanEck SMH ETF — all closed by Q4.
